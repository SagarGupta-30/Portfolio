FEATURED HEALTHCARE DESIGN CONCEPT

Maternal Care & Appointment Guide

A self-initiated healthcare communication concept demonstrating how thoughtful graphic design makes complex medical instructions approachable, empathetic, and stress-free for expectant mothers and families.

Accessible Hierarchy

Bold headers and scannable milestones help patients quickly identify essential appointment-day actions.

Reassuring Palette

Calming soft teal and warm ivory reduce clinical anxiety while maintaining strict WCAG contrast standards.

Actionable Checklists

Chunked checkboxes ensure patients feel prepared with required documents, tests, and support arrangements.

Empathetic Iconography

Gentle line icons provide immediate visual cues across diverse language and literacy backgrounds.

Note on Concept: Self-initiated design concept demonstrating patient communication design principles for digital healthcare organizations like immidit. Not a commissioned or commercial assignment.
